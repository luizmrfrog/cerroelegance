Colors

Cerro Élégance mapped onto the same roles. Primary #657159, secondary #BFB091, tertiary #8B5F40, quaternary #ECE9DF. Ink, graphite and white stay: they carry type and shadow, not the brand. The darker olive is the primary deepened, so buttons and the featured card keep their depth. Small primary text sits on shell or white (4.7:1); on the bare offwhite ground it is 4.3:1, so body copy there stays ink. Source of truth: tokens.css.

Foundations · 03

Backgrounds

Three grounds: the hero's layered paper with pointer-steered rain (also the live ground of this whole view), the closing section's falling petals, and the page frame that holds everything.

Hero ground · rain

#top · #bloom · canvas#heroParticles (2d)

Layers: shell paper rgb(247,245,239) · charcoal smoke (blur 70px) · primary bloom (blur 60px, follows the pointer ±13px / ±10px over 0.7s) · floor haze · rain canvas. Rain: 70 drops (40 under 768px), 12–20px/frame, wind follows the pointer's x, 35% of new drops spawn near the pointer, drops splash on the floor and on the hero's title, tiles, links and risers. Click the ground to open or close the umbrella; it follows the pointer and drops splash on its dome.

Closing ground · petals

footer#contact · canvas#footerParticles (2d)

Petals: 35 quadratic-curve petals in four warm sands, gravity 0.8, wind 1.2, sine sway, slow spin; the loop only runs while the section intersects the viewport. Glow: olive radial from the bottom edge, blur 60px.

Page frame

body (rgb(236,233,223)) › div.p-2.sm:p-3.lg:p-4 › div.rounded-[28px].sm:rounded-[40px]

The whole page lives inside one rounded paper shell (overflow hidden) inset 8 / 12 / 16px from the ground.
